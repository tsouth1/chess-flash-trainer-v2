## Core concept

The application contains memory exercises using a chessboard and colored chess pieces.

The player must:

1. Click “Start”.
2. Observe the chessboard and memorize the positions of the pieces.
3. Click “OK” to hide or alter the board according to the selected exercise.
4. Reconstruct the original or transformed piece positions.
5. Click “OK” again to submit the answer.
6. Receive immediate feedback showing whether the answer was correct.
7. Optionally click “Answer” to reveal the correct solution.

The pieces are memory-game objects, not standard chess pieces. Their movement does not need to follow chess rules unless required by the selected exercise.

## Exercise types

Implement the following game modes.

### 1. Statics

- Display a chessboard with randomly positioned colored pieces.
- The player clicks “Start” to begin.
- The player memorizes the positions.
- When the player clicks “OK”, remove all pieces from the board.
- The player drags pieces back onto their original squares.
- The player clicks “OK” to submit.
- Compare the player’s arrangement with the original arrangement.
- Show correct and incorrect results.
- Display score, elapsed time, current level, and attempt number.

### 2. Transposition

- Display a chessboard with randomly positioned colored pieces.
- After the player clicks the first “OK”, change the positions of the pieces once.
- Allow the player to drag the pieces back to their original positions.
- Let the player submit the answer.
- Show the correct original arrangement and the player’s result.
- Clearly indicate which pieces are misplaced.

### 3. Flashes

- Display a chessboard with pieces.
- Continuously or repeatedly change the positions of the pieces.
- The player must observe the board and decide when all pieces have returned to the initial arrangement.
- Provide a button labeled “OK” for submitting the answer.
- Include configurable flash speed and number of flashes.
- Avoid making the exercise impossible or frustrating by providing reasonable defaults.
- Highlight whether the player submitted at the correct moment.

### 4. Moves

This mode is available only in advanced editions.

- Initially show the chessboard and pieces.
- After the player clicks “OK”, hide the chessboard.
- Display a list describing how each piece moves.
- Each move must include:
  - Piece identifier
  - Number of squares
  - Direction
- The player mentally calculates the new positions.
- After clicking “OK”, hide the move list and show the board again.
- The player places the pieces on their calculated new positions.
- The player clicks “OK” to submit.
- Validate the final positions and show the result.

Use directions such as:

- Up
- Down
- Left
- Right
- Up-left
- Up-right
- Down-left
- Down-right

Keep pieces inside the board boundaries.

### 5. Super Moves

This mode is available only in advanced editions.

- Initially show the chessboard and pieces.
- After the first click on “OK”, hide the board and show the movement instructions.
- After the second click on “OK”, hide the movement instructions.
- Do not show the chessboard again automatically.
- Instead, show a position-selection interface for each piece.
- Let the player select coordinates such as “A1”, “E2”, or “H8”.
- Require the player to select a new position for every piece.
- After all positions are selected, allow the player to click “OK”.
- Validate the final positions.
- Clearly remind the player about the orientation of letters and numbers on the board.
